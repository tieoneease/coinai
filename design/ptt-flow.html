What the diagrams assume

Press → recording targets: Active < 50 ms · Standby ≤ 400 ms · Off ≤ 700 ms + PMIC ON-time (128 ms–2 s).

Why PWR is the mic button: it is the only key that can power the board on from Off, and its state reaches the ESP32 on GPIO3 (high while pressed), an RTC pin that can wake deep sleep.

Why Standby drops the BLE link: the board has no 32 kHz crystal, so holding a connection in light sleep keeps the 40 MHz crystal running. Reconnecting after wake is cheaper.

Android app wake: the system sees the bonded coin advertising (Companion Device presence) and binds the app's CompanionDeviceService even if the app isn't running. That service starts a short-lived connectedDevice foreground service to sync, which shows a notification only while syncing.

Network: the companion app is exempt from battery optimization, so it can upload during Doze. Processing runs in WorkManager and retries when offline.

Out of range: clips stay on flash and transfer on the next connection, resuming from the last byte offset.

    U->>D: release PWR
    D-->>A: CLIP_ENDED (size, CRC)
    A->>D: ACK, so the device deletes its copy
    A->>S: process: upload via WorkManager, or on the phone
    D->>D: disconnect, back to Standby
